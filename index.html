📚 Cyberbullying Awareness

What is Cyberbullying?

Cyberbullying is bullying that happens over digital devices through SMS, social media, forums, or gaming platforms. It includes sending, posting, or sharing negative, harmful, false, or mean content about someone else.

Warning Signs

Unexplained anger after device use, reluctance to discuss online activities, avoiding school, sudden grade drops, appearing upset after being online.

The T.H.I.N.K Test

Before you post, ask: Is it True? Helpful? Inspiring? Necessary? Kind? If not, don't send it.

Be an Upstander!

Support victims, refuse to forward harmful messages, report the content, and encourage the victim to seek help. Don't be a bystander.
